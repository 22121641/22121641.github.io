Unit 8 mobile app development

In this unit what I had to do was plan, design and test a mobile app that I created for children between the ages of 3-7 years old and the mobile app would teach them the first 6 letters of the alphabet also the shape and sounds of the letters and be able to draw on the screen aswell.

In this video It will show you my finished Mobile App that consists of a start screen the letters A-F of the alphabet and then an end screen. You will also be able to listen to the sounds of the coresponding letter depending on the screen you are on and you will also be able to draw on the screen also when you get to the end you can restart the Mobile app and go back to the beginning again.
