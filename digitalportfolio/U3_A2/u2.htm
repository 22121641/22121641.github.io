Another topic that I learned about was Computer networking and this taught me about the different networks like LAN witch stands for local area network and you would use this network in your home, schools or office buildings. Another example would be PAN witch stands for personal area network you would use this for wireless speakers for bluetoth connection or a smartwatch and only has a short connection.
