Static Discharge

In this unit I also learned about the dangers of electro static discharge this is an ongoing problem for anyone when working with the Hardware componnents of a desktop computer because the build up of static electricity can damage the internal componnents of a computer. To avoid this happening and it being a costly mistake I learned on my course that you have to ground yourself by touching bare metal before handling the internal componnents of the computer. Other methods to prevent static electricity is to use a anti static mat or even gloves to prevent costly mistakes when handling the internal componnents of a computer.
